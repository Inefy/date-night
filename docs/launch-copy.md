# Date Night Deck Launch Copy

## One-Sentence Pitch

Date Night Deck helps couples draw thoughtful, low-pressure date ideas that fit their mood, budget, energy, and real life.

## App Store Name

Date Night Deck

## Subtitle

Draw better plans together

## Short Description

Draw a date card, tune it to your mood, and turn "what should we do?" into a plan that feels easy to say yes to.

## Long Description

Date Night Deck is a playful planning tool for couples who want more intentional time together without making date night feel like another project.

Choose a budget, energy level, weather mode, food preference, and vibe, then draw a curated date card with a complete plan: premise, itinerary, prep list, twist, backup option, and a conversation prompt when you want one.

Keep it cozy at home, find something spontaneous, rescue a rainy evening, make a first date less awkward, or shake up a routine that has gone a little quiet. You can save favorites, remix ideas that are too far or too much effort, add plans to your calendar, and lock a mystery card for your partner to reveal.

Date Night Deck is built for practical romance: clear plans, warm prompts, flexible options, and no pressure to spend big, dress up, drink, or perform a perfect version of date night.

## Onboarding Value Props

- Pick your default date style once, then draw faster next time.
- Set budget and energy so plans fit the night you actually have.
- Choose indoor, outdoor, or flexible ideas without sharing exact location.
- Add food and dietary preferences, or skip food-focused plans entirely.
- Use solo mode first, then invite a partner when it feels useful.
- Skip anything you do not want to answer yet.

## Review Prompt Timing Recommendation

Ask for a review after a user has had a meaningful success moment, not during setup. Best timing for the MVP:

1. After a user saves at least two date plans.
2. Or after a user reveals a mystery date and taps "I'm in."
3. Or after a calendar event is created successfully.

Avoid prompting immediately after sign-in, onboarding, failed sync, denied permissions, or account setup. If the user dismisses the prompt, wait at least several weeks and another positive action before asking again.

## Privacy Positioning

Date Night Deck does not need exact location for the MVP. Location preferences are simple modes like at home, out, hybrid, or virtual.

Couple data is private to the couple profile. Saved dates, mystery cards, feedback, and shared preferences should be treated as personal relationship context, not marketing data.

Analytics should stay minimal and practical: track non-sensitive product events like date generated, date saved, or mystery revealed. Do not store personal messages, private prompts, exact plans, exact location, or sensitive relationship details in analytics properties.

The Supabase publishable key is acceptable in the client only when Row Level Security is enforced. Service role keys, secret keys, and other privileged secrets must never be included in the app bundle, documentation examples, screenshots, or build profiles.
